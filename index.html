The I Am Sayings of Jesus

Seven times in John’s Gospel, Jesus tells us who He is — and every time, it is for us.

“Most certainly, I tell you, before Abraham came into existence, I AM.”

John 8:58

When God met Moses at the burning bush and Moses asked His name, God answered: I AM. It is the name of the One who simply is — eternal, unchanging, needing nothing.

Centuries later, Jesus of Nazareth took that same name onto His own lips. And He did not leave it bare. Again and again He filled it with pictures drawn from ordinary life — bread, light, a door, a shepherd, a vine — so that we would know not only that He is God, but what He is for us.

Here are the seven sayings, in His own words.

01

The Bread of Life

“I am the bread of life. He who comes to me will not be hungry, and he who believes in me will never be thirsty.”

John 6:35

Underneath all our hungers is one deeper hunger — for Him. Come to Him, and that deepest ache is fed: not once, but every day, and forever.

✦ ✦ ✦
02

The Light of the World

“I am the light of the world. He who follows me will not walk in the darkness, but will have the light of life.”

John 8:12

You do not have to find your own way through the dark. Follow Him, and the road ahead is lit — not by your own strength, but by the light of life itself.

✦ ✦ ✦
03

The Door

“I am the door. If anyone enters in by me, he will be saved, and will go in and go out, and will find pasture.”

John 10:9

There is one door into safety, and it stands open. Whoever enters by Him is home — saved, free, and led out to pasture.

✦ ✦ ✦
04

The Good Shepherd

“I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.”

John 10:11

A hired hand looks after himself; this Shepherd looks after His sheep at the cost of His own life. You are not watched over from a distance — you are laid down for.

✦ ✦ ✦
05

The Resurrection and the Life

“I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in me will still live, even if he dies.”

John 11:25

He did not promise that a resurrection would happen someday — He said He is the resurrection. For anyone who believes in Him, death is real, but it is not the end.

✦ ✦ ✦
06

The Way, the Truth, and the Life

“I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father, except through me.”

John 14:6

When you do not know the way, He does not hand you a map and leave you to it — He is the way. Trust Him, and you are already on the road home to the Father.

✦ ✦ ✦
07

The True Vine

“I am the true vine, and my Father is the farmer.”

“I am the vine. You are the branches. He who remains in me and I in him bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing.”

John 15:1, 5

Your life does not depend on how tightly you can hold on, but on staying joined to Him. Remain in Him, and His life flows into yours, bearing fruit you could never grow on your own.

✦ ✦ ✦

Every Time, For Us

Seven times He says who He is, and every time it is for us: bread for the hungry, light for the lost, a door standing open, a shepherd who lays down his life, resurrection and life itself, the way home to the Father, and a vine that keeps us alive. He is all of it — because He is I AM.
